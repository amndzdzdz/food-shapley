## Questions this week's entries should answer

- Which claims had to be weakened, qualified, or withdrawn during writing or
  internal review, and on what grounds?
- Did any figure fail to regenerate from its config? That is a reproducibility
  defect and should be recorded, not just fixed.
- Did any number in the draft disagree with the run artifacts? Record the
  discrepancy and its cause.
- Which venue was selected, and what constraints did it impose on scope?
- What reviewer objections were anticipated, and which ones could not yet be
  answered with evidence?

Also update the status of earlier entries: anything a Week-2 result no longer
supports should be marked `contradicted` or `superseded` rather than quietly
edited.
