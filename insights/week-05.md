## Questions this week's entries should answer

- **H5.1** How does the interaction-based index relate to SugarCrepe and ARO
  accuracy across models? State *n* — with a handful of models the correlation
  is weak evidence either way, and the entry must say so.
- Is the index just accuracy in disguise? Record the evidence for *and*
  against.
- For each model, were benchmark numbers measured here or sourced from
  publications? Mixing sources is a caveat that must be recorded.
- **H5.2** Does single-part reliance predict misclassification on the shifted
  set?
- **H5.3** Does the interaction-based diagnostic beat the plain-saliency
  baseline? Without this comparison recorded, no generalization claim may be
  made — this is the direct response to Viviano et al. (ICLR 2021).
- How was the shifted/harder test set constructed, with what seed and selection
  criteria, and what confounds does it introduce?

A null result on H5.2 or H5.3 is a legitimate finding and should be written up
with the same care as a positive one.
